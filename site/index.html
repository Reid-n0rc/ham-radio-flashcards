Printing

Eight 4 × 2½ inch cards per sheet. The back of each sheet is mirrored so it lines up when your printer flips the page.

  1. Set up duplex

    Letter paper, double-sided, flip on long edge. Card stock works if your printer takes it.

  2. Print at actual size

    Turn off “fit to page” or “shrink to fit”. Scaling moves the backs out of line.

  3. Test one sheet

    Print pages 1–2 and hold the sheet up to a light. The dashed lines on both sides should overlap.

  4. Print and cut

    Print the rest, then cut along the dashed lines. The marks in the margins guide a paper cutter.

Which pool you’ll be tested on

Each pool is used for exams from July 1 of its first year through June 30 of its last.

Checked every day

An automated job compares these cards against the NCVEC Question Pool Committee’s release pages, including errata.

Withdrawn questions come off

When NCVEC withdraws a question, it is removed from the cards on the next run and new PDFs are published.

Every change is recorded

Each rebuild becomes a release with the PDFs attached. Changes to NCVEC’s pool pages open an issue for review.

Question pools are released into the public domain by the NCVEC Question Pool Committee. This project is not affiliated with NCVEC or ARRL. Check ncvec.org for the official pools.
